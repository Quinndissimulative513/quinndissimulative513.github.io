The platform offers several ways to look at your career data.

*   **Salary Benchmarking:** Compare your pay against global averages. 
*   **Predictive Modeling:** See how your skills and experience affect your income.
*   **Anomaly Detection:** Find outliers in salary trends by region or role.
*   **Segmentation:** Identify industry clusters that pay higher premiums.
*   **Interactive Dashboard:** Explore data points through a web interface.
*   **Report Generation:** Access a 17-page analysis in Power BI format.

## ⚠️ Troubleshooting

If you experience issues during setup or usage, follow these tips:

*   **Application stays frozen:** Close the window and restart the program. Ensure no other heavy software runs in the background.
*   **Charts fail to display:** Check your internet connection. Although the software runs on your computer, it needs a connection to initialize the dashboard components.
*   **Installation errors:** Run the installer as an administrator. Right-click the file and select "Run as administrator" from the menu.
*   **Missing fonts or icons:** Update your Windows installation to the latest version. This ensures that all required display libraries exist on your machine.

If the problem persists, ensure your antivirus software does not block the application. You can add an exception for this folder in your security settings to prevent false alarms. The software contains no malicious code or trackers.
